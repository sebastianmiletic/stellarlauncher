# Stellar identity

Original Stellar Launcher artwork, created as editable SVG for this project. Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0, matching the upstream artwork license. Existing Prism artwork and notices remain attributed to their original authors.

- `archive-mark.svg`: editable square master.
- `archive-logo.png`: 1024px export with transparency outside the mark.
- `archive-wordmark.svg`: horizontal dark-surface wordmark.
- `app.archivelauncher.ArchiveLauncher.svg`: embedded app mark.
- `archive-launcher.icns`, `archive-launcher.ico`, `archive.iconset/`: OS exports.

- `stellar-wallpaper.svg`: monochrome black-hole artwork used in the launcher and Minecraft title screen.

The mark pairs a dark event horizon with a single white accretion ring. The launcher reveals the wallpaper from the bottom during game startup.
